# Kuwala Books features

What's live, what's waiting, and what's next. Last updated 25 October 2026.

## Live

### Buying
- Catalogue from our own database, then Google Books, then Open Library, then manual entry. Covers copied to R2.
- Search: exact words, close spellings and AI reading of requests ("grade 7 maths in Bemba"); instant results in the header; eBay-style filters.
- Book pages: every copy for sale from every shop with photos of each copy, delivery estimate to the buyer's town, a trust strip (delivery, payment, protection), details table, about the author, more by this author, reviews with the average beside the title, share on WhatsApp.
- Bag grouped by shop; checkout with mobile money or card (Lenco); escrow until the buyer confirms delivery.
- Delivery: two prices, K50 in town and K100 out of town plus K10 per extra book (Mercury premium, collect free); each shop's parcel priced from its own town; free delivery over a shop's threshold.
- eBay-style offers with one counter each side, a hidden floor and 24-hour replies.
- Zambia Top 20 (Zambian authors, distinct buyers, weekly), shown only once there is real data.
- School book lists: photo or typed list read by AI and matched to the catalogue.
- Prices in ZMW, USD display only.

### Selling
- Start selling flow: Reader, Author, Bookshop or Publisher, worded for each; progress saved on the device; share screen at the end.
- Listing flow with ISBN or title lookup, photo reading by AI, price guide, open to offers; up to three photos of the copy, required for used copies.
- Shop page with photo or logo, rating, WhatsApp share.
- Payouts: delivered sales collect into a balance, paid free every Friday (K100 or more, or after 30 days); Pay me now sends it at once for Lenco's fee.
- Sales page with shipping labels and buyer standing.

### Authors
- Author pages: bio, links, books, verified mark, milestones, readers count, share, "Buy direct" from the author's shop.
- Verification: NRC or passport, a selfie with the book, one listed book, mobile-money name match. Documents in a private bucket, deleted after review.
- Milestones: Copper (25 readers), Emerald (250), Gold (1,000), kept once earned, emailed, celebrated once on the account. Need a 4.0 average once an author has 10 reviews.
- Claim your page: on Zambian authors' pages, straight into verification.
- AI-researched author pages (admin): web research, sources shown, admin publishes, invite by email or WhatsApp with a single-use link and opt-out.

### Trust and reputation
- Blind two-way ratings after delivery (stars and tags).
- Book reviews from verified buyers; phone numbers and links blocked; admin can hide.
- Reader badges (Page turner, Bookworm, Kuwala patron) and Trusted reader, shown to sellers only.
- One reminder to rate and review, 3 days after delivery.
- Phone numbers kept out of offer messages; contact shared only after payment.

### Admin
- Overview, orders and disputes, sellers, books (with delete), reviews, authors, verification queue, author drafts, publishers, delivery rates.

### Brand and platform
- kb mark for icons and share previews, wordmark in the header and emails, book-photo fallbacks for shops.
- Installable PWA, offline page, light and dark mode, low-data covers.

## Built, not yet proven end to end

- Checkout delivery dropdown with books from two shops.
